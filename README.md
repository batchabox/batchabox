# Batchabox

**Your agent has its tools. These are yours.** *(From anywhere.)* A native Mac app for checking what your agent wrote: Git, files and databases in one window, every card linked to where it came from. Pair your iPhone or iPad once and open the same projects from anywhere, even your localhost.

**[Download for Mac](https://updates.batchabox.app/Batchabox-latest.dmg)** · **[iPhone and iPad on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/batchabox/id6832096188)** · [Linux, early access](https://batchabox.app/linux) · [Docs](https://batchabox.app/docs) · [Release notes](https://batchabox.app/releases)

- **Agent worktrees as a review queue:** Conductor, worktrunk, gtr or your own pattern, with what needs you now.
- **Git review:** any branch against its base, word by word. Stage a hunk, commit, push, cherry-pick, resolve conflicts.
- **Databases:** PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB and SQLite, found in your `.env`. Edit cells in the grid, saved as one transaction, with a prod pill.
- **Code and files:** syntax colours, go to definition, find in files, quick fixes.
- **Browser:** your local dev site on the iPhone and iPad, through the paired connection.
- **Native and fast:** Swift and AppKit, 0.35 s to a window, 119 fps scrolling, ~150 MB at rest.
- **Private:** no telemetry, no account. Your code and credentials stay on your machines.

Free for personal use. Always.

## This repo

The public issue tracker. The app is closed source, so there is no code here, only issues.

- **A bug:** [report it](https://github.com/batchabox/batchabox/issues/new?template=bug.yml)
- **An idea:** [suggest it](https://github.com/batchabox/batchabox/issues/new?template=feature.yml)
- **A security problem:** please don't open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Issues here are public. Never paste passwords, keys, connection strings, or rows from a real database.
