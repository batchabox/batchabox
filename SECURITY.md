# Security

Found a way into someone's box, their data, or their pairing? Please tell us privately. Don't open a public issue.

- Use [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/batchabox/batchabox/security/advisories/new) on this repo, or
- write to security@batchabox.app.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version you tried (on the Mac: Settings → Updates). We answer within a few days, keep you posted while we fix it, and credit you in the release notes unless you'd rather not be named.

Fixes go to everyone, free, whatever their license.
